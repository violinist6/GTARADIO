GTA V Radio

This player runs the radio on your phone. It needs the radio file made by GTARadio.exe on your PC (GTARadio-phone.pack).

  1. On your PC, click Make iPhone copy in the radio page.
  2. Get the file onto this iPhone, for example in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone in the Files app.
  3. Tap the button below and pick it. It's copied into this app, so you can delete the original afterwards.

Add this page to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there. Safari can clear the storage of pages that aren't on the Home Screen.
